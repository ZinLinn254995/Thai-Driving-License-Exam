About Thai Drive Exam

Thai Drive Exam is an independent practice website intended to help learners review road signs, traffic laws, safe driving, vehicle maintenance, and accident-prevention topics before studying for a Thai driving licence test.

The site provides English-first practice questions with optional Myanmar (Burmese) translations to help Myanmar-speaking learners understand the wording of questions and answer choices while preparing for a Thai driving licence test. Learners can practise in English and reveal the Myanmar translation when they need extra support.

It is not operated by, approved by, or affiliated with Thailand’s Department of Land Transport (DLT), thaidriveexam.com, or any government agency.

Question sources and accuracy

The question bank includes material sourced from thaidriveexam.com, with translations and presentation changes for learners. Source credit does not itself establish permission to reproduce material. Rights and source attribution must be respected; if you own material shown here and have a concern, please use the contact page to report it.

Questions, translations, images, and answer explanations may contain errors or become outdated. Use this website as a study aid, not as an official answer key. Always check current rules and official guidance before driving.
