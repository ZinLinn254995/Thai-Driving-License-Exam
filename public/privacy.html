This policy explains how Thai Driving Test Practice handles information when you use this website.

Information stored on your device

The practice app may store your language preference, bookmarks and exam history in your browser's local storage so these features continue to work on the same device. This information is stored in your browser and is not an account profile maintained by this website. Clearing browser storage may remove it.

Accounts and direct collection

The current practice tool does not require you to create an account to answer questions. Do not submit sensitive personal information through public issue trackers or other third-party links.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

This policy must be updated if analytics or advertising services are enabled. If Google AdSense is used in the future, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure and personalize ads as permitted by applicable settings and law. Users can learn about Google's advertising practices and controls at Google Ads policies and controls. The website should not be considered to be serving AdSense ads unless and until that service is actually configured.

Third-party services

Links to third-party websites are provided for convenience. Their privacy practices are controlled by those providers, not by this website. Please review their policies before sharing information.

Children and contact

This is a general educational tool, not designed to collect personal information from children. For privacy questions or requests, use the contact route on our Contact page.

Policy updates

This policy may be revised when website features or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
