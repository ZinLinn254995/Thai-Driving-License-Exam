Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This page explains what the current version of Thai Drive Exam does with information when you use the website.

Information stored on your device

The practice app uses your browser’s local storage to remember selected language preferences, bookmarks, and practice/exam history. This information is stored in your browser on your device; the app does not currently require an account to use these features. Clearing browser site data may remove these saved items.

External services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those font files, Google may receive technical request information such as your IP address, as described in Google’s own privacy documentation.

Advertising and analytics

At the time this policy was written, this codebase does not include an active Google AdSense integration or a site analytics script. If advertising, analytics, or other data-collecting services are added, this policy must be updated to describe them before they are enabled. Third-party services have their own policies and settings.

Your choices

You can remove locally saved preferences and practice history by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings. You can also choose not to use the website.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use the project contact link on the Contact page.
