Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This page explains what the current version of Thai Drive Exam does with information when you use the website.

Information stored on your device

The app uses your browser’s local storage to remember your Myanmar-translation preference, sound preference, bookmarked question IDs, and practice/exam history. These settings and records are saved in your browser on your device. The current app does not require an account for these features, and the code reviewed for this policy does not send this local history to an app-owned account or cloud service. If you use a shared device, other people with access to that browser profile may be able to see saved history or bookmarks.

Practice history is limited to the 30 most recent attempts in the current implementation. Browser storage can be unavailable, blocked, or cleared; in those cases, preferences or history may not persist. Avoid using the app on a device or browser profile where you do not want this information saved.

External services

The site requests font files from Google Fonts. When those files are requested, Google may receive technical request information, such as your IP address and browser details. Review Google’s Privacy Policy for information about Google’s handling of data.

Cookies, advertising, and analytics

The website includes an optional Google AdSense integration. Advertising code is loaded only when the site owner configures a valid AdSense publisher ID and ad-unit IDs; without those settings, no AdSense script or ad units are loaded. If ads are enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies and process technical information (which may include IP address, browser/device information, and ad interactions) to serve ads, limit repeated ads, measure performance, prevent fraud, and, where enabled and permitted, personalize advertising.

Google’s use of information is subject to its own policies and settings. Read Google’s advertising technologies information and Google My Ad Center to learn about available ad-personalization controls. Third-party vendors may use information according to their own privacy policies. This website does not control those external practices.

Advertising is not yet considered active merely because this integration exists in the code. Before enabling ads for visitors, the site owner must confirm the applicable consent requirements, configure any required consent-management solution for the audiences served, publish the correct AdSense publisher information, and review this policy for the actual configuration and applicable law.

Your choices

You can remove saved preferences, bookmarks, and practice history by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings. Doing so may also reset other site preferences. You may also choose not to use the website. External services are subject to their own privacy policies and controls.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, visit the Contact page. The current contact method links to a public GitHub issue tracker. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue; use only information needed to describe the problem.
