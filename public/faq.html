Frequently Asked Questions

These answers explain how to use this independent Thai driving-test practice website. They are not official licensing advice. For a step-by-step learning routine, read the Thai driving-test study guide.

What is Thai Drive Exam?

Thai Drive Exam is an independent study tool for reviewing traffic signs, traffic laws, safe driving, vehicle maintenance, and accident-prevention topics. It is not operated by or affiliated with Thailand’s Department of Land Transport (DLT) or another government agency.

Are these the exact official exam questions?

This website does not guarantee that its questions are identical to the current official test. Question wording, translations, images, answers, and exam formats may be incomplete, outdated, or inaccurate. Use the practice tool to support learning, and confirm current licensing requirements and rules with the relevant Thai authorities.

How should I use Practice Mode and timed practice?

Use topic-based study first to understand concepts and identify weak areas. Review mistakes before moving on. Once you feel more comfortable, timed practice can help you practise concentration and pacing. A score in this app measures only your performance in this practice session; it does not guarantee an official exam result.

Can I read questions in English and Myanmar?

The main interface and original practice wording are in English. Use the Show Myanmar button to reveal Myanmar (Burmese) translations for supported quiz questions and answer choices. This feature is intended to help Myanmar-speaking learners understand the material while practising English; it does not turn the site into an official bilingual exam resource. Translations are learning aids and may not capture every nuance. If an English question and its translation seem inconsistent, do not assume either version is authoritative—verify the underlying rule and report the issue through the Contact page.

Does the website save my progress?

The current app stores supported preferences, bookmarks, and recent practice history in browser storage on your device. These records may not transfer to another browser or device, and clearing the site's browser data may remove them. On a shared device, other people using the same browser profile may be able to see saved information. Read the Privacy Policy for more detail.

Why should I review mistakes instead of only repeating full tests?

Repeating tests can improve familiarity, but a high score alone may hide misunderstandings. After a wrong answer, try to explain the safety principle or rule in your own words. Revisit the topic later and check whether you can still explain it without relying on memorized wording.

What should I do if a sign image or answer looks wrong?

Do not memorize content that appears inaccurate. Note the question number and describe what seems incorrect, then submit a report through the Contact / Report an issue page. This helps identify possible errors without silently treating uncertain content as verified fact.

Does a passing practice score mean I will pass the official test?

No. The score and threshold shown by this website are practice feedback only. Official requirements, procedures, and test formats may differ or change. Check current information directly with Thailand’s Department of Land Transport before your appointment.

Is the website free to use?

Yes. The current practice features are available without a paid subscription.

How can I report a copyright, privacy, or accessibility concern?

Use the Contact page to report a concern with the page URL or question number where possible. Avoid posting private or sensitive personal information in public reports. For more about the site and its limitations, see About and the Study Disclaimer.
