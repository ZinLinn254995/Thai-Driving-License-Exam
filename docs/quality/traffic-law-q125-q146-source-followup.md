# Traffic-law source follow-up: Q125–Q146

Audit branch only. No quiz content changed.

- Q125: Thai `การย้ายรถ` refers to notifying the registrar when a vehicle is relocated, not necessarily transferring ownership. English “moving/transferring” conflates two different procedures; Myanmar wording is also broad. Separate relocation from ownership transfer and cite the applicable registration rule before changing the answer.
- Q129: The 25 cm keyed answer is supported by Land Traffic Act B.E. 2522, section 54: park parallel and close to the curb/shoulder, with distance not exceeding 25 cm. Source: https://lawplayer.com/th/act/pythainlp_f36542870dba846f
- Q146: The 30 m keyed answer is supported by section 51 for positioning in the appropriate lane before turning where lanes/signs apply. The current stem is a simplified teaching summary; preserve the caveat that lane markings and bus-lane restrictions matter. Source: https://lawplayer.com/th/act/pythainlp_f36542870dba846f
- Q132: “Most common cause of traffic accidents” is an unscoped statistical superlative. The keyed speeding answer needs a named source, time period, geography, and definition of cause before being presented as universally true.
- Q134: The keyed drink-driving penalty is presented without offence context. Confirm the current statutory wording and whether the question means a first offence; do not imply this is the penalty for every circumstance.
