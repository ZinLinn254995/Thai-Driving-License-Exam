## Q171 — Eligibility for a private driver's licence
- Current keyed answer: Mr. Chai, who is not physically disabled to the extent of being unable to drive.
- Finding: Supported as the best answer among the choices, but the question is a simplified single-condition test. Thailand Motor Vehicle Act, section 46 lists multiple qualifications/disqualifications: age, driving knowledge/ability, disability that makes driving impossible, a disease a medical practitioner considers dangerous while driving, mental illness as specified, existing same-type licence, and a licence under seizure/suspension/revocation conditions.
- Myanmar translation: Meaning is broadly aligned with the English. No change made.
- Status: Key appears reasonable among the given options; question wording is incomplete if interpreted as a full list of eligibility requirements.

## Q173 — Can a person with epilepsy drive?
- Current keyed answer: “No, in any case.”
- Finding: Overly absolute as a general medical/legal statement. Section 46 of the Motor Vehicle Act disqualifies a person with a disease that a medical practitioner considers may be dangerous while driving; the text reviewed does not itself establish that every person with epilepsy is automatically barred in every circumstance. Risk depends on condition and applicable licensing/medical rules.
- Myanmar translation: Accurately conveys the absolute meaning of the English, but that absolute meaning is the concern.
- Status: MANUAL REVIEW — possible overgeneralization in the question/answer. Do not edit without approval.

## Q174 — Can a person with diabetes drive?
- Current keyed answer: Yes, if there are no symptoms that risk loss of consciousness or inability to control the vehicle.
- Finding: The safety principle is plausible, but the answer is not confirmed as a complete Thai DLT rule. The statutory framework also considers whether a medical practitioner judges a disease dangerous while driving. The wording may omit relevant risks such as hypoglycaemia, medication effects, and individual medical assessment.
- Myanmar translation: Broadly matches the English answer.
- Status: MANUAL REVIEW for legal/medical completeness; no change made.
